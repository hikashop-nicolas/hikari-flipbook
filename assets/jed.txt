**Features**
Turns a PDF, a folder of images, or a mix of both into a real page-flip book on your Joomla site.
Renders the PDF in the browser with Mozilla PDF.js, so nothing is uploaded to a third-party service and the pages stay crisp at any zoom level.
Single page, double page, and automatic switching to single page on phones and narrow screens.
Zoom with the mouse wheel, pinch, or double tap, then pan around the page.
Navigation with previous, next, first, last, a page number box, and the keyboard arrows.
Inline display in a module position or an article, lightbox display, and native fullscreen.
Direct links to a page, so you can point a visitor straight at page 12.
Optional page-turn sound, configurable flip speed, colours, container height, and right-to-left reading.
Every toolbar button can be shown or hidden individually, including the download button.
Fully keyboard accessible with screen-reader page semantics, and it honours the reduced-motion setting of the visitor.
Search inside the document, with an outline panel and a thumbnail strip.
Include a book from a module position, from an article with a shortcode, or from a custom field on any article, contact or product.
No jQuery, no external requests, no account, no watermark, no page limit.

**Installation**
1) Download the package from our website
2) Install it via the Joomla Extension Manager
3) Publish the module in a position, or insert the shortcode in an article
