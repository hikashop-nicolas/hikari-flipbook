A book, not a picture of one

This is the front-end both packages ship, loaded exactly the way the Joomla module and the WordPress shortcode load it. The document is a PDF sitting next to this page; nothing is uploaded and no server does any work.

Drag a corner to turn a page, or use the toolbar. Arrow keys, Home and End work once the book has focus.

  • Search the document from the toolbar
  • Contents and pages from the button beside it
  • Two hotspots are outlined on pages 2 and 3
  • Sound plays from the first turn a visitor makes
