A book made of
web pages

Every page of this book is an ordinary HTML file in a folder. The text is real text, the links are real links, and the pages are edited the way any other page on the site is edited: no export, no regeneration, no PDF.

Turn the page with the arrows, the keyboard, or by dragging a corner.

A folder of pages is the fourth kind of book this viewer reads, beside a PDF, a folder of images, and an EPUB.
